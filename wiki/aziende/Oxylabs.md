# Oxylabs



Lithuanian startup that became an AI data powerhouse by turning its decade-old proxy and web-scraping network into infrastructure that helps AI models and agents access live internet data at scale. Recently secured $130 million from Warburg Pincus at a $3.6 billion valuation after reaching $350 million in annual recurring revenue.
